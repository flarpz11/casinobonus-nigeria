Why Does 1Win Ask for Verification?

KYC (Know Your Customer) is a standard compliance requirement under 1Win's Curacao license 8048/JAZ 2018-040 — not something unique to Nigerian accounts.

Before Your First Withdrawal

Most accounts are asked to verify identity the first time they request a payout, to confirm the funds go to the account owner.

Large or Unusual Transactions

Bigger deposits or withdrawals can trigger an additional identity and source-of-funds check.

Anti-Fraud & AML Compliance

Licensed operators are legally required to verify players to prevent fraud and money laundering.

Common Rejection Reasons & How to Fix Them

Blurry or Cropped Photo

Retake the photo in good lighting on a flat surface. Make sure all four corners of the document are visible in the frame.

Expired Document

1Win only accepts currently valid IDs. Check the expiry date before uploading and use an alternative document if needed.

Name Mismatch

The name on your ID must match the name on your 1Win registration exactly. Contact support to correct your profile details if they differ.
